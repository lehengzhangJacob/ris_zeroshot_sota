\section{Introduction}

Referring image segmentation (RIS) takes an image and a free-form referring
expression and returns the mask of the described object. In the training-free
or zero-shot regime, where no pixel-level supervision and no task-specific
fine-tuning are used, essentially every published method follows the same
template: a frozen class-agnostic segmenter --- almost always SAM
\cite{kirillov2023sam} --- emits a pool of candidate masks, a frozen
vision--language model --- almost always CLIP \cite{radford2021clip} --- scores
each candidate against the expression, and the highest-scoring candidate is
returned. Differences between methods lie in how the score is formed
\cite{yu2023global,liu2025hybridgl,wu2026glodris}, in which additional cues are
introduced \cite{kim2023refdiff,li2023tas}, or in whether an external
multimodal model is consulted \cite{li2025lgd}.

The organising assumption behind this body of work is that the \emph{proposal
pool} is the limiting factor, and that progress consists of obtaining better
masks, better features, or better auxiliary supervision. The assumption is
rarely tested. When it is, the test is usually an oracle utilisation figure ---
the ratio of achieved IoU to the best IoU available in the pool --- which is
reported as evidence that the segmenter has room to improve, without asking
which part of the discarded IoU was discarded \emph{by the selector} and why.
GLOD-RIS \cite{wu2026glodris}, for instance, reports an oracle utilisation of
roughly 62--65\,\% oIoU and concludes that the ceiling is mask recall; an
oracle utilisation of 62\,\% equally means that 38\,\% of the IoU that
\emph{was} available was thrown away at selection time.

This paper asks the question in a form that can be settled by measurement:
\emph{when a training-free RIS pipeline fails, is the failure in the pool or in
the selector, and if it is in the selector, what kind of selection error is it
and is it repairable without training?}

We answer it by decomposing the oracle gap --- the difference between the IoU
of the best pool member and the IoU of the returned mask --- into attributable
classes, using only quantities that the pipeline already computes. The
decomposition (§\ref{sec:diagnosis}) yields one dominant class. \emph{Wrong
instance}: the selector returns a mask that is very nearly disjoint from the
referent but belongs to the correct category. It carries 82.2\,\% of the
recoverable IoU at the unguided operating point and 77.4\,\% at the released
HybridGL operating point on RefCOCO val, and 86.4\,\% / 80.1\,\% at the
released point on RefCOCOg / RefCOCO$+$ respectively. Under-segmentation ---
the granularity class that mask-merging and mask-refinement remedies target ---
carries 14.3\,\% and 17.6\,\% at the two full-split RefCOCO operating points
and never exceeds 22.6\,\% in any configuration we measured. Wrong-instance is
overwhelmingly a \emph{spatial reference} failure: 84\,\% of those
expressions contain a spatial word, against 67\,\% of the split overall when
probed with the same broad lexical list.

The mechanism follows from the diagnosis. The released HybridGL pipeline does
contain spatial machinery --- a relationship-guidance term and a GEM attention
$\times$ position-mask coherence term --- but it applies both only inside
$\mathrm{topk}(\text{score},3)$ under the full expression, so its answer is
always one of the three highest-scoring proposals. On the wrong-instance
failures the referent is inside that set only 30.2\,\% of the time (mean
score-rank 9.1). Yet a perfect chooser restricted to the released top-3 would
already reach 0.5812 mIoU against the 0.4482 achieved, and the referent does
reach the top-8 of a \emph{category} score --- the same scorer reading the
expression with its spatial vocabulary removed --- for 70.1\,\% of those
failures.

That is an actionable structural statement, and §\ref{sec:method} acts on it
with a training-free operator that changes only the candidate set and the
final selection step: score proposals with the category score, take its top
$k$, and if the expression carries a spatial relation, return the member the
relation selects, ranked by the pipeline's own coherence score. The operator
adds no training, no ground truth, and no forward pass.

Our contributions are:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A decomposition of the oracle gap in training-free RIS}
(§\ref{sec:diagnosis}), showing that wrong-instance selection rather than mask
quality carries 77--86\,\% of all recoverable IoU, measured on the full
validation splits of RefCOCO, RefCOCO$+$ and RefCOCOg, at two operating points
on RefCOCO and one on RefCOCO$+$/g, and surviving a doubling of proposal
density.
\item \textbf{A mechanism-level account of why the released guidance cannot
repair it} (§\ref{sec:diagnosis}), in terms of a measurable confinement of the
pipeline's answer to a top-3 candidate set, together with the ceiling that
confinement imposes.
\item \textbf{A training-free operator that does repair a substantial part of
it} (§\ref{sec:method}, §\ref{sec:experiments}), raising full RefCOCO val from
0.4482 to 0.5029 mIoU and 0.3816 to 0.4431 oIoU over the released pipeline,
with the gain concentrated exactly where the diagnosis predicts.
\item \textbf{A negative-result set and a measured boundary} (§\ref{sec:experiments}, §\ref{sec:limitations}): the operator's
mIoU gain is statistically indistinguishable from a one-line containment snap;
it is neutral on RefCOCOg and RefCOCO$+$, where spatial expressions are 3--36$\times$
rarer; an MLLM chooser baseline was not run and is disclosed as the largest
open flank.
\end{itemize}

We deliberately report the boundary of the repair rather than tuning it away.
The diagnostic is the durable contribution; the operator is what the diagnostic
buys on the benchmark where spatial reference is common, and RefCOCO+ ---
collected with location words excluded --- is used as a \emph{pre-registered}
test of that reading.
