\section{Method: Category-Set Relation Guidance}
\label{sec:method}

The diagnosis says the binding constraint is the candidate set the released
guidance is allowed to act on, and that the category score generates a better
set than the full expression. The operator follows directly. It replaces the
final selection step and nothing else; it introduces no parameters, performs no
training, uses no ground truth, and adds no forward pass --- every quantity it
consumes is already computed by the pipeline it is applied to.

\subsection{The operator}

Given an image $x$, an expression $e$, the proposal pool $M$ with per-proposal
semantic scores, the released coherence score $c_i$, and mask geometry:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Category score.} Evaluate the frozen text encoder on $e$ with its
spatial vocabulary removed, and score every proposal against it. This is one
text encoding per expression, not one per proposal.
\item \textbf{Candidate set.} Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the top-$k$ proposals under
the category score ($k=8$ throughout).
\item \textbf{Relation parse.} Parse $e$ deterministically for a spatial
relation: a horizontal direction (\emph{left}/\emph{right}), a vertical
direction (\emph{top}/\emph{bottom}/\emph{above}/\emph{below}), a centrality
term (\emph{middle}/\emph{centre}), or an ordinal with an axis (\emph{second
from the left}).
\item \textbf{Selection.} If a relation was parsed and $|\mathcal{C}|\ge 2$,
return the member of $\mathcal{C}$ the relation selects: for an ordinal, the
$n$-th proposal along the relation axis by mask centroid; otherwise the
proposal with the highest released coherence score $c_i$ among those in the
half of $\mathcal{C}$ lying on the relation's side, split at the median along
the relation axis. If no relation was parsed, or $\mathcal{C}$ is a singleton,
return the pipeline's own selection unchanged.
\end{enumerate}

Two design choices carry the mechanism and are ablated in
§\ref{sec:controls}. Ranking is by the \emph{released coherence score}, not by
the semantic score and not by geometry: the coherence term already encodes the
pipeline's own spatial prior, and §\ref{sec:controls} shows that replacing it
with the semantic score removes the gain entirely. The side constraint is
taken relative to the candidate set's own median rather than the image centre,
so the operator asks ``which of these candidates is the left one'', not ``which
candidate is in the left half of the image''.

\subsection{Ordinal expressions abstain}

A deterministic ordinal resolver was implemented and measured: it returns the
$n$-th candidate along the relation axis. On its stratum ($n=444$ on RefCOCO
val) it \emph{loses} 0.153 mIoU relative to the released selection, so the
shipped operator abstains on ordinal expressions and the stratum is reported as
an open problem rather than silently absorbed. Ordinal reference is 4.1\,\% of
the split.

\subsection{Cost}

The operator adds one text encoding per expression, an $O(N\log k)$ partial
sort, an $O(k)$ split, and $O(k)$ lookups. It is $O(k)$ per expression after
the encoding the pipeline already performs, and it is a pure post-processing
step: it can be applied to a stored score matrix without re-running any model,
which is how all results below are produced.
