\section{Appendix: implementation details}
\label{app:impl}

\paragraph{Scoring pass.}
For every expression we store: the released semantic score under four text
variants (full expression, spatial-stripped, content-only, head noun phrase),
the negative-noun score, the released GEM coherence score, per-proposal mask
area, centroid and bounding box, the strict-containment parent of every
proposal, the pool pairwise IoU matrix, and the per-proposal IoU against
ground truth (the union of the reference's masks on gRefCOCO). Everything
else in this paper is a re-analysis of these records; the records themselves
come from one forward pass per dataset
(\texttt{exp/exp4\_guided\_full.py}, \texttt{exp/exp21\_grefcoco\_full.py}).

\paragraph{Shipped configuration.}
Candidate base $=$ rank$(S^{\mathrm{dec}})+\gamma\,$rank$(S^{\mathrm{coh}})$
with $\gamma=0.49$; $k=15$; overlap mode $=$ IoU; selection $=$ argmax of the
mode over the candidates; growth off. These five values are the best of
\studyTrials{} Optuna trials on the held-out split, objective $=$ minimum over
datasets of mIoU (\texttt{exp/out\_final/optuna\_study\_sent.json}); the
noun-phrase study is the second file. The earlier repairs (relation-gated
operator with $k=8$ and a two-axis gate, containment growth at
$|A\cap B|/|A|\ge0.95$) are reported as ablations and are off in the shipped
configuration.

\paragraph{Bounding-box prior.}
Prompt: ``Locate the \{expression\} in this image. Reply with ONLY the
bounding box as [x0,y0,x1,y1] in pixel coordinates measured from the
top-left corner, or the single word none if it does not appear in the
image.'', with the full expression substituted. Decoding is greedy,
\texttt{max\_new\_tokens}{}$=32$; the reply is parsed for its first four
numbers and values $\le1.5$ are read as normalised coordinates. Boxes are
cached per (image, expression) under \texttt{exp/out\_vlm\_boxes/}; the
vision tower pass is cached per image. The 8-bit backend
(\texttt{exp/convert\_llavaov\_hf.py} converts the cached checkpoint to the
HF layout so the standard bitsandbytes path applies) produced the same boxes
as bf16 on our agreement sample.

\paragraph{GRES extension.}
Clause split on top-level ``and''/``or''/``,''; one box per clause; per-clause
selection by Eq.~\ref{eq:select}; union of the selected masks; empty output
when every clause answers \texttt{none}.

\paragraph{Compute.}
All scoring passes ran on 4$\times$RTX~4090. Per dataset the pass costs
about 0.9--1.2 GPU-hours; the entire paper (four datasets, plus controls and
ablations, which are CPU re-analyses) is under 6 GPU-hours.

\paragraph{Val-pass ledger and reproduction pins.}
Every arm in the learned-prior table and the transfer-inversion study is a
single frozen application of the full official val split. Within the
transfer-inversion study that is five new configurations (recipe,
recipe$+$combination policy, strata-$\{1,2\}$, weights-only, tau-only), seven
pre-registered leave-one-rare-ref-out refits (diagnostics only; the shipped
row was declared unchanged on any outcome), and two bit-reproduction passes
for the cluster bootstrap; the weak-signal democracy probe ran on a seeded
$800$-row val sample under its own single-pick protocol (its number is a
sample mIoU, never a full-val cIoU). Arm, threshold and weight selection used
the held-out train carve-out only. Reproducing the shipped recipe bit-exactly
requires \texttt{OMP\_NUM\_THREADS=4} (as the shipped runs used): under a
different thread count the logistic-regression fits drift in the last decimal,
two margin-boundary rows flip their rank-1 second part, and the train table
rebuilds with $10{,}352$ parts instead of $10{,}351$ (val $52.684$ vs
$52.706$); under the pin an independent control refit reproduces the shipped
row exactly.

\paragraph{Term-alignment audit and the official metric.}
gRefCOCO refs group several sentences (14{,}229 val records over 4{,}237
ref\_ids) and the official evaluator scores each sentence separately. We
imported the released ReLA \texttt{ReferEvaluator} with a minimal
detectron2 stub and ran a five-case battery (targeted exact/wrong/abstain-FP,
no-target TP/FN); its accumulated cIoU/gIoU/N-acc/T-acc arithmetic matches
ours, with one convention difference: a targeted false positive in the
official path keeps the discarded mask's union in the cIoU denominator, while
our empty-prediction abstention contributes only the GT area. The audit also
showed that our gRefCOCO term lookups were keyed by ref\_id rather than by the
row's own sentence, so 84.7\% of targeted rows in multi-sentence refs used
another sentence's terms. All gRefCOCO term-path results in this paper are
re-evaluated with per-record alignment
(\texttt{gref\_tf\_aligned.json}, \texttt{gref\_transfer\_recipe\_aligned.json},
\texttt{gref\_search\_val\_part0.json}); the RefCOCO-family path uses
per-record text and is unaffected. The corrected frozen detector-bearing rule is
49.63/74.60 cIoU/gIoU; a held-out re-selection reaches 50.34/74.69 (retained as
the detector-bearing ablation). The declared-trained recipe measures
49.40/74.26 and is detector-dependent, so it is not shipped. With GroundingDINO
removed, the VLM-box SAM rule reaches 47.59/73.78 and is the shipped gRefCOCO
row; the earlier promotion is withdrawn.

\section{Appendix: independent GLOD-RIS reimplementation}
\label{app:repro}
GLOD-RIS's code is unreleased. To record what the described method reaches
under this harness, we reimplemented it end to end on the same frozen stack as
our baselines (SAM ViT-H candidates, CLIP ViT-B/16 scoring) and queried its
own MLLM, GLM-4.1V-Thinking-Flash, through the Zhipu API with our own prompt;
every response is cached, and parsing and transport failures are parked and
counted rather than aborting a run ($15$, $14$ and $0$ expressions of the
three splits ran the guidance-free path); degenerate answers are handled
explicitly rather than silently (an all-zero grid becomes a uniform,
uninformative localization map -- $92$, $169$ and $64$ expressions -- and a
category-less answer drops only the category term, which never triggered). The
reimplementation completes on all three official val splits;
Table~\ref{tab:repro} records its full-pipeline numbers alongside the
published val mIoU (oIoU: $50.85$, $45.96$, $50.03$) for reference.

Two measurements bound what this record covers.
(i)~\emph{Candidate generation is not the limit}: the oracle mIoU over the SAM
pool is $82.2$; under our reading of the published equations the largest
component effect is the Gaussian-grid localization term, whose removal raises
RefCOCO val from $32.68$ to $36.11$\,mIoU, while the category term adds about
$+1$\,mIoU and the orthogonal decoupling about $+0.7$
(Table~\ref{tab:repro}). These are properties of this reimplementation.
(ii)~\emph{Feature-path anchor}: the released ancestor scores $44.82$ on the
same split in this harness while the guidance-free reimplementation reaches
$35.09$; the two are different systems, so the $\approx\!10$\,mIoU between
them is an anchor rather than a controlled isolation. It bounds the largest
single identified contributor; the remainder is not attributed, and may
include differences between our prompt and the authors' GLM usage and
components their unreleased code contains. The published rows remain the
comparison in the main text; this appendix is a transparency record of our
reading of the description, not a measurement of the original work.
