\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The same-class floors are cleared; the supervised cIoU level is the measured boundary.}
The training-free rule clears every same-class (zero-shot) published number
on both metrics (\grefCIoU{}/\grefGIoU{} vs.\ the best published zero-shot
\grefZeroShotCIoU{}/\grefZeroShotGIoU{}), exceeds the strongest supervised
gIoU reference (\grefPubGIoU), and makes the no-target decision above the
trained state of the art ($N_{\mathrm{acc}}=\wallCombN$ vs.\
\wallTrainedNAcc). The \emph{supervised} cIoU level
(\grefPubCIoU, our labelled reference rows) is where the class stops.
\emph{Grounder}:
\wallTwoTarget\,\% of gRefCOCO's targeted samples union exactly two objects,
and the VLM's single box overlaps that union at IoU \wallBoxIoU.
\emph{Selector}: at the shipped operating point the supervised cIoU level
needs the deployable-oracle realization at \wallRealNeedCIoU\,\% while the
rule realizes \wallRealMeas\,\%; both oracles agree at $\approx0.88$ --- pool
selection and the construction itself under per-object GT boxes and SAM
(reaching $r=\wallConstructionOrc$, cIoU \wallConstructionCap) --- so the
axis is realization-bound, not foreclosed, and the held-out 2$\times$2 shows
the loss concentrates in \emph{which box to ask} ($+0.23$ mean targeted IoU
with GT boxes against $+0.07$ for perfect mask choice). The gap is not
perceptual-only: a forced-choice probe of the localizer between two candidate
crops picks GT-best at $29.8\%$ against $25\%$ chance on the held-out grid, and
on the two-object slice the same protocol reaches $59.3\%$ --- below the
trivial largest-candidate heuristic ($85.4\%$), so the preference carries less
referent information than crop size. Published cIoU numbers
come from models trained to select at that quality. We report our
training-free numbers honestly and do not tune around the gap.

\paragraph{The gRefCOCO wall is measured on val, and part of it is transfer.}
On gIoU the held-out train population reaches $\grefSelHeldOutGIoU$ at val
prevalence while val gives $\grefGIoU$ --- the same transfer component
documented throughout this paper (val's no-target expressions are 92\,\%
transplanted phrases; the shipped rule's $N_{\mathrm{acc}}$ drops from
$\wallHeldOutNAcc$ to $\wallCombN$). The cIoU boundary inherits this: the
$r$-requirement is measured on val and the held-out realization is higher,
so the val gap understates what the rule achieves on train-distributed data
and overstates nothing --- we state the transfer component separately so the
boundary is not read as more absolute than the measurement supports.

\paragraph{One VLM, one prompt template.}
The prior comes from a single frozen 7B checkpoint (LLaVA-OneVision-7B) with
one prompt template, chosen on the held-out split between two prompt
variants. We did not sweep models, templates, few-shot examples, or decoding
parameters; the reported prompt effect ($+13.4$/$+10.6$/$+11.6$ mIoU for
sentence over noun phrase) suggests the choice matters a great deal and may
not be optimal. On gRefCOCO the localizer's \emph{discrimination} is the
measured limit, not the question format: asked for a box it overlaps a
two-object union at IoU \wallBoxIoU; asked yes/no about a candidate crop it
confirms but cannot reject; asked to choose between two candidate crops it
picks the GT-best at $29.8\%$ against $25\%$ chance on the held-out grid
(20{,}301 queries), reaching $59.3\%$ on the two-object slice --- below the
$85.4\%$ of the trivial largest-candidate heuristic. The one additional strong
VLM we could obtain and verify (Qwen2.5-VL-7B, 2026-10-07) improved on this
checkpoint's boxes ($0.580\to0.6325$ term-box IoU) but stayed below the
detector already in the configuration ($0.7046$) and its forced-choice
discrimination was below the incumbent's, so a stronger grounder would
plausibly help every number, but not trivially. The weaker-lineup audit below
additionally measures a \emph{smaller} 3B prior from the same Qwen family,
which outperforms the shipped checkpoint --- the family's grounding advantage,
not its size, is what the measurement isolates.

\paragraph{The VLM is pretrained on COCO images.}
LLaVA-OneVision's pretraining data includes COCO (the source of the
benchmark images), as is true for the MLLM that GLOD-RIS queries through its
API; no referring annotations are used by either. This is the standard
training-free setting in this literature, but it is a weaker notion of
``zero-shot'' than a model that has never seen the image pool.

\paragraph{Which backbones are load-bearing: a measured weaker-lineup audit.}
The shipped lineup (SAM ViT-H, CLIP ViT-B/16, LLaVA-OneVision-7B boxes, the
learned head; plus GroundingDINO on the gRefCOCO path only) was re-measured
under strictly weaker replacements on the full val splits, each under a head
refit on train-carved rows wherever the platform changed
(\texttt{backbone\_audit.json}). Two components can be weakened for free ---
one of them profitably: the box prior gets \emph{smaller and better}
(Qwen2.5-VL-3B, 3.8B, holds full-table SOTA and improves on the 7B boxes:
$73.57$/$67.64$/$66.43$ vs $70.57$/$66.35$/$65.51$\,mIoU --- in this
two-point comparison the advantage tracks the model family, not its size;
a within-family size sweep is not measured), and replacing the released
G2L region-text platform with our own plain masked-region CLIP features (no
fusion, no complement blur) costs only
$0.34$/$0.37$/$0.19$\,mIoU ($70.22$/$65.98$/$65.32$) while keeping SOTA. Two
boundaries are real and measured: with SAM ViT-B (0.09B) the pool's own oracle
drops from $79.1$ to $71.4$\,mIoU on RefCOCOg and the refit head lands
$57.57$, $0.28$ \emph{below} the published GLOD-RIS floor --- a margin that
is within unquantified single-seed refit noise, so the load-bearing
conclusion rests on the pool-oracle gap, not on the $0.28$ (the ViT-L point
between them is unmeasured: its original-format checkpoint was unreachable
offline, its HF port restructure being incompatible); and the detector-free gRefCOCO realization
(LLaVA boxes only, which necessarily drops the trained recipe: its candidate
substrate is detector-derived) fails the same-class cIoU floor
($38.71$ vs $44.4$), so the detector-bearing path is load-bearing there ---
scoped to that realization, the intermediate arm being unmeasured.
The boundaries are reported per component, not tuned around. The pool-density
axis was additionally varied
($\textsc{points\_per\_side}$ $8\to16$, \ppsProposalDelta\,\% more proposals)
and the diagnosis survives
(\ppsWiEight\,$\to$\,\ppsWiSixteen).

\paragraph{Reproduction gap.}
Absolute rows are not comparable to the published HybridGL table: the
released code scores \reproGap\,mIoU below its own published number
(\S\ref{sec:repro}), verified against the unmodified official
implementation. All our comparisons are internal to one reproduction. This
constrains the paper: we can claim controlled improvements over the released
pipeline and against published numbers quoted as published, but the two are
not directly commensurable.

\paragraph{VLM latency.}
The box pass costs $0.6$--$1.8$\,s per expression on one RTX 4090, which is
$3$--$10\times$ the released pipeline's per-expression cost. The cost is
cacheable (one box per unique expression) but not free, and the paper does
not report a wall-clock comparison against the MLLM-based prior it replaces.
