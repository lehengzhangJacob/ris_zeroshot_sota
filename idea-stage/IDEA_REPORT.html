Literature Landscape

The zero-shot RIS literature was mapped from the 9 local PDFs plus 8 downloaded arXiv papers. Every method in the field is a propose-then-select pipeline over a frozen SAM pool scored by a frozen CLIP, with globally-constant fusion weights. The tables and findings below establish the landscape, the one structural regularity, and the five attack surfaces named in the brief.

The task and its two regimes

RIS takes an image plus a free-form referring expression and returns a mask. The zero-shot / training-free regime — no pixel-level supervision, no task-specific fine-tuning — is universally built as propose-then-select: a frozen class-agnostic segmenter (almost always SAM) emits a candidate pool, a frozen vision-language model (almost always CLIP) scores the candidates, and the best candidate is returned.

Nine papers were read in full from papers/ (GLOD-RIS, HybridGL, BMS, IteRPrimE, TAS, LGD, Ref-Diff, CaR, MCCE-REC) and eight more were downloaded from arXiv (see Downloads below).

The structural finding: every one of them is a single-proposal argmax

Across all ten methods the output is argmax_i S(m_i) over one candidate mask. The fusion weights that combine the sub-scores are global constants, never instance-adaptive: HybridGL α=0.6, λ∈{3,9,14} hand-switched by mask size; GLOD-RIS μ,τ fixed and λ = clamp(area, μ, τ); TAS α=0.1, λ∈{0.7,1.0} per dataset; BMS one α per image from a Wasserstein heuristic; LGD α∈{0.5,0.3} per dataset. No method calibrates its score across the candidate pool of the image it is scoring.

GLOD-RIS's own numbers say selection, not proposals, is the bottleneck

This is the single most decision-relevant piece of evidence, and it comes from the reference paper itself:

  • Fig. 6: oracle utilisation (achieved IoU ÷ best-possible-proposal IoU) is only ~62–65 % oIoU and ~70–73 % mIoU, stable across SAM backbones. The paper concludes the ceiling is SAM's mask recall — but an oracle utilisation of 62 % means 38 % of the available IoU is thrown away by the selector.
  • Table VII, grid granularity: oIoU RefCOCO val is 19.35 / 50.85 / 25.13 for 2×2 / 3×3 / 4×4. A genuine granularity trade-off is smooth; a drop to ⅓ of the score at 2×2 and ½ at 4×4 is a prompt-format cliff, i.e. the MLLM localisation signal is largely an artefact of one grid format.

The local independent reproduction independently confirms both, and adds an operational failure the paper cannot show: the full RefCOCO-val run aborted at 223 / 10834 expressions with GLM invalid response: Grid must contain exactly n*n binary values.

Where the field is genuinely empty (Novelty-relevant)

Two independent literature sweeps (17 papers, incl. the 2025 Multimodal Referring Segmentation survey, GRES/gRefCOCO, and GREx/IJCV) found:

  • No zero-shot or training-free method has ever been evaluated on gRefCOCO / GRES. The survey's GRES table has exactly one zero-shot entry (LaSagnA, a trained assistant evaluated zero-shot); GREx states outright that SAM was excluded from the GRES benchmark because it "has not yet released its text prompt".
  • No paper performs an oracle / proposal-recall ceiling analysis — the words oracle, upper bound, ceiling do not appear in the survey, GRES, or GREx.
  • No zero-shot method handles ordinal, depth, or relational expressions; GREx lists counting/ordinal as open future work.
  • Closest prior art: RAS (ICCV 2025) selects an arbitrary subset of frozen SAM proposals for a mask group — but with a learned binary selection head trained on ~2M mask groups and a fine-tuned LLaVA-1.5-13B (≈3.5 GPU-days on 8×A100), and it never performs a pixel-wise union. Grounded-SAM unions SAM masks for all detected boxes, training-free, but has no per-referent selection, no no-target handling, and has never been run on gRefCOCO.

🏆 Idea 1 (RECOMMENDED) — "What is actually lost in zero-shot RIS selection? A proposal-pool diagnostic"

Type: analysis / diagnostic contribution, not a method. Hypothesis: in training-free zero-shot RIS the proposal pool is not the bottleneck; the selector is, and the loss is caused by the near-flat region-text score landscape rather than by any fixable weighting of existing cues.

Measured support (all new measurements; none found in a 17-paper sweep):

Idea 3 (BACKUP) — HybridGL-guided replication of the two headline numbers

Re-measure only oracle recovery and score flatness / rank-of-best inside the full released HybridGL pipeline at its published 0.4948 mIoU operating point. ~1 GPU-day. This is the external-validity gate: if the flatness persists at the guided operating point the diagnostic generalises; if not, every claim must be scoped to the unguided point.

Idea 4 (ELIMINATED by pilot) — Seed-Anchored Gated Growth (SAGG)

Gate proposals within tolerance of the top score, then take the connected growth of the argmax seed. Initially the strongest-looking result: +0.0615 mIoU (95 % CI [+0.047,+0.077], p<0.0001, win/loss/tie 264/112/340), replicated on a fresh seed with new images (+0.0545) and on the plain HybridGL scorer (+0.0674). Killed by controls:

  • parent_strict — a one-line rule (snap the argmax to the smallest SAM proposal containing ≥95 % of it) — reproduces 98 % of the gain (+0.0654 vs +0.0670).
  • The gain is zero on the smallest GT-area quartile (-0.0009) and +0.134 on the largest → a granularity correction, not a selection mechanism.
  • It is negative on part expressions (-0.0110), exactly as predicted.
  • The gate tolerance peaks sharply at the published 0.95 constant (+0.0670) and falls to +0.0179 at 0.99 → a tuned threshold, not a robust principle.
  • Dilation and "largest gated" do not reproduce it, so it is not raw area inflation — it is specifically hierarchy snapping, which is a known SAM post-processing move.

Reviewer verdict: ABANDON the method claim. Recorded; the operator survives only as the explanatory control inside Idea 1.

Idea 5 (ELIMINATED by pilot) — Unsupervised per-expression cue re-weighting

Weight each score cue by its agreement with the other cues on that expression. Hurts: 0.3263 vs 0.3532 baseline. The per-expression oracle over 972 weight settings reaches 0.5215 (63 %), but that is a maximum over many near-random reorderings of a 0.01-wide score spread and is an inflated estimate, not an achievable target.

Idea 6 (ELIMINATED by pilot) — Parser-free soft spatial cue

Detect direction words by CLIP text-embedding similarity and apply the preference to mask centroids — the MLLM-free, parser-free replacement candidate for GLOD-RIS's 3×3 grid. Near-random alone: 7.5 % of oracle. Fires on 36 % of expressions.

What survived and what did not. The proposed merge operator turned out to be the connected component of the argmax seed in the overlap graph of gated masks, which is not a new algorithm, and its gain was then shown to be a granularity artifact. The novelty that does survive is the diagnostic package — in particular the pool-level flatness measurement (~0.01 cosine separating IoU 0.00 from 0.93 over a proposal pool) and the oracle-recovery quantification, neither of which the sweep could find in any published work, plus the bounded-headroom result for the entire composition family.

External Critical Review

Two review rounds were required: the first rejected the method scope, the second accepted the re-scoped analysis. Both verdicts and their binding conditions are recorded below.

Cross-family review (mcp__llm-chat__review, reviewer glm-5.3, executor deepseek-flash), two threads: llm-chat:2d66e8eb427a + llm-chat:cdb7beff0221 (method scope, ABANDON), and llm-chat:0b326b8c12df (re-scoped analysis, PROCEED).

Round 1 (method scope) — bottom line: abandon. The reviewer's decisive objection was that every positive result was obtained on a deliberately weakened scorer, and that the gain might be a granularity artifact of the near-tie regime that stripping the guidance modules created. The controls in Idea 4 then confirmed it. The reviewer's own pre-registered decision rule was: on the full-strength HybridGL scorer over complete RefCOCO val, upgrade only if Δ ≥ +2 mIoU with a CI excluding 0, otherwise abandon as a method paper.

Round 2 (analysis scope) — bottom line: PROCEED. Verbatim verdict and conditions:

  • THIN: yes as-is. "The taxonomy decomposition (granularity vs over-segmentation vs wrong-instance vs correct) is the one measurement that converts 'selector discards 58 % of oracle' into an attributable, actionable claim and ties findings 1, 2 and 4 together."
  • MISSING: the error taxonomy on the full 10,834-expression val split; secondarily, replicate only the two headline numbers (oracle recovery, score flatness / rank-of-best) inside full HybridGL — "a day of compute, not a full replication."
  • VENUE: workshop paper (CVPR/ICCV vision-language-understanding or referring-expression workshop) or the analysis section of a larger method paper. Main-conference analysis track only with the HybridGL headline check plus one more dataset/backbone configuration.
  • MUST NOT CLAIM: "that the 42 %-oracle-recovery / flat-selection pathology characterizes training-free RIS at the SOTA operating point — every flatness, ceiling, and headroom number is measured on the guidance-stripped scorer (0.3501 vs 0.4948) and must be scoped as 'at the unguided operating point matched to HybridGL's L-alone ablation', never generalized to guided pipelines or stated as a field-wide result."

Also accepted from the review: the oracle figure (0.8285) is scorer-independent — it is a property of (image, GT, proposal pool) — so finding 1 is fully robust to the stripped-guidance objection; only the recovery ratio (42.3 %) is operating-point-dependent. This distinction must be kept explicit in the write-up.

Open risk carried forward: if the guided replication shows the score is no longer flat at the full HybridGL operating point, the diagnostic must be re-scoped to the unguided regime and the "field-wide" framing dropped entirely.
