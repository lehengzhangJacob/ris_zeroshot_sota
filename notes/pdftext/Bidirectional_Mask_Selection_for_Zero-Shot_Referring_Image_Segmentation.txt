                                                                                    The main contributions of our work are summarized as
                                                                                 follows:
                                                                                    • To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work to
                                                                                      incorporate the negative mask into zero-shot referring
                                                                                      image segmentation, which makes full use of the semantic
                                                                                      context information around the target semantics to guide
                                                                                      the referring image segmentation.
                                                                                    • We propose a novel mask adaptive fusion strategy
                                                                                      to combine the complementary information from pos-
                                                                                      itive and negative masks without additional training,
                                                                                      which enhances the robustness of zero-shot referential
                                                                                      segmentation.
                                                                                    • Extensive experimental results across three databases val-
                                                                                      idate the effectiveness of our approach. Specifically, our
                                                                                      zero-shot BMS has even surpassed the existing weakly
                                                                                      supervised methods on the RefCOCOg dataset.
